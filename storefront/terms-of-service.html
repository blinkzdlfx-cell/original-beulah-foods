Submitting checkout information creates a pending order and a temporary stock reservation when the requested items are available. A reservation is not a completed purchase. Unpaid reservations can expire and release the held stock.

Payment is processed through Paystack. A successful redirect alone is not treated as proof of payment; payment status is verified before the order is finalized. Promo codes are subject to their configured eligibility rules, including active dates, usage limits, minimum order requirements, and discount limits where configured. The final discount is determined by the server-side checkout rules.

Delivery availability and fees are determined by the current store settings shown or applied during checkout. Customers should provide accurate delivery information so an order can be fulfilled.

7. Cancellations and failed payments

Unpaid orders may be cancelled or expire according to the checkout state. Failed or abandoned payment attempts do not constitute successful purchases. Where payment status is unclear, the transaction may need verification before any order is treated as paid.
