Privacy Policy

How Beulah Foods handles information used to provide the online store.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we may process information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, and account activity needed to provide the service.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate customers, process and deliver orders, manage reservations and stock, apply eligible promotions, provide customer support, and communicate about transactions or account activity.

3. Payments

Online payments are processed through Paystack. Beulah Foods does not ask customers to submit their card details directly to the Beulah Foods website. Payment status is verified through the payment provider before an order is finalized.

4. Storage and service providers

Application data is stored and processed using the services that operate the Beulah Foods application, including Supabase and Cloudflare. Product images are stored in the application's public product-image storage area.

5. Browser storage

The storefront may use browser storage for application functions such as maintaining the shopping cart and authentication session. This information is used to operate the site and is not a substitute for the server-side order records.

6. Security

Access controls, authentication, database authorization policies, and server-side payment processing are used to protect application data. Sensitive payment and backend secrets are not placed in the customer-facing application.

7. Data requests

If you need to ask about information associated with your Beulah Foods account or order, contact us using the customer support contact published on the website.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
